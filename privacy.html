Privacy

Last updated: November 2026 · applies to OverGrow for Mac and the OverGrow Themes community site

OverGrow collects nothing, connects to nothing, and stores everything on your Mac.

What the app does

OverGrow measures how long your screen has been in use, using the system's built-in idle-time information and the name of the frontmost application. Both are read locally, on your Mac, without any special permissions. This information never leaves your device: the app has no network access at all.

What is stored, and where

  • On your Mac only: stare-time history, your Garden progress, settings and theme preferences — saved as local files.
  • In your Apple account only: if you buy the one-time upgrade, the purchase record is handled by Apple as part of your App Store account. We never see your name, email, or payment details.

What we don't do

  • No accounts. No sign-in anywhere.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers.
  • No camera or microphone access, ever.
  • No third-party trackers on this website.

The community site

Theme submissions and discussions are hosted by GitHub under GitHub's own privacy policy. If you submit a theme, you choose what contact information to include; publish only what you're comfortable sharing.

Payments and refunds

The one-time upgrade is billed and refunded entirely through Apple's App Store. For anything payment-related, start with Apple's purchase history; we have no access to it.

Questions

Write to hello@overgrow.app. Because the app holds no personal data, there is usually nothing to delete — uninstalling removes everything.
